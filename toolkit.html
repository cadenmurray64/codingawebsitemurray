Typography of Design

01

Choosing a Font

Typography is the foundation of visual communication, and understanding type categories helps designers choose fonts with confidence. Serif, sans‑serif,and decorative typefaces each carry distinct personalities that shape how a message feels and functions. By recognizing these categories and the moods they convey, designers can select fonts that support clarity, tone, and purpose across any project.

02

Font Pairing

Font pairing is basically about choosing two fonts that look good together and make your design easier to read. Usually, you pick one font for the main text and another for the headings so there’s a clear difference between them. When the fonts match in vibe but still have contrast, your design looks cleaner, more organized, and way more professional.

02 / Principles

Principles of Design

01

Color

The most effective website and app color scheme will follow a 60-30-10 ratio. This means that the main color is applied to 60% of the website design, the secondary color is applied to a further 30%, and the last 10% is used as the accent color that contrasts with the two main colors.

02

Contrast

Contrast is a basic design principle that helps important parts of a layout stand out. You create contrast by changing things like size, color, weight, or spacing so one element looks different from the others. This makes the design easier to read and helps guide the viewer’s eyes to what matters most. When contrast is used well, the whole design feels clearer and more organized.

More about Contrast
03

Negative Space

Negative space is the empty or blank area around the main parts of a design. Even though it looks like “nothing,” it actually helps make the important elements stand out and keeps the layout from feeling crowded. When negative space is used well, the design becomes cleaner, easier to read, and more visually balanced. It basically gives your content room to breathe.
